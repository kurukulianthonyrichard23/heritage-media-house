2. How to Book a Photoshoot

Booking with Heritage Media House is simple. Follow these steps: Step 1: Choose Your Service Select the type of photography you need, such as portrait, graduation, wedding, birthday, family, or event photography. Step 2: Select Your Preferred Date Choose the date you would like your photoshoot to take place. Step 3: Provide Your Details Enter your name, phone number, email address, and any other information needed to arrange your session. Step 4: Choose a Location Tell us your preferred photoshoot location so that we can discuss the arrangements. Step 5: Confirm Your Booking Submit your booking request and wait for confirmation of availability, pricing, and other details.

3. Booking Information

To help us organise your photoshoot, please provide the following information: - Full name - Phone number - Email address, if available - Photography service required - Preferred date and time - Photoshoot location - Additional requests or special instructions Please provide accurate information so that we can communicate with you about your booking.

4. Our Photography Packages

Heritage Media House aims to provide photography services for different occasions and budgets. Basic Package: Suitable for simple personal portraits and small photoshoots. Standard Package: Suitable for graduation pictures, birthdays, and family photography. Premium Package: Suitable for larger events and clients who require additional photography and editing services. Prices depend on the service, location, duration, and editing requirements. Contact us for package details and a quotation.

5. Booking Terms and Conditions

Before booking a photoshoot, please take note of the following: - Bookings are subject to availability. - Clients should provide accurate contact information. - The date and time must be confirmed before the photoshoot. - Any changes to a booking should be communicated in advance. - Prices and any applicable payment arrangements should be agreed upon before the session. - Clients should communicate any special photography requirements before the photoshoot.
